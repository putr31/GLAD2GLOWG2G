## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an airy, approachable, yet premium dermatological beauty aesthetic. Designed for Gen-Z and millennial skincare enthusiasts seeking playful efficacy, it pairs clean clinical confidence with soft, welcoming radiance. The experience should evoke feelings of dewy freshness, gentle comfort, and daily self-care rituals.

The aesthetic fuses **Soft Modernism** with delicate **Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-clean porcelain surfaces accented with whipped rose and luminous peach tones.
- Deep slate and warm charcoal typography that provides high-contrast legibility without the sterile severity of pure black.
- Tactile, pillowy UI geometry with generous rounded contours and soft ambient rose-tinted shadows, replicating the sensory feel of smooth jars, emulsions, and cloud-creams.

## Colors

The palette revolves around radiant floral undertones anchored by balanced, warm neutrals:

- **Primary (`#EE729D`):** The signature vibrant rose accent, utilized for primary calls-to-action, key promotional highlights, and selected states.
- **Secondary (`#FFB4A2`):** Soft peach tone providing warmth across secondary visual accents, sub-banners, and warm glowing highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#FF758F`):** Deep coral-rose for hover dynamics, focus rings, and high-impact micro-elements.
- **Neutral (`#374151`):** Calibrated deep slate providing crisp readability across body copy and secondary headers, intentionally replacing harsh `#000000`.
- **Surface Roles:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the base canvas for product photography clarity, layered over delicate tinted fills (`#FFF0F3` and `#FFE5EC`) for structural framing and card containers. Rosy structural strokes (`#FCE7F3` and `#F4CCD3`) create whisper-soft boundaries.
- **Compliance & Credential Accents:** Emerald tones (`#059669` on `#ECFDF5`) validate official safety claims (Halal, BPOM, Dermatologist Approved) without conflicting with the pink brand foundation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the friendly, balanced geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the meticulous legibility of **Inter**:

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Labels):** Provides an open, youthful, editorial voice. Letterforms use slight optical negative tracking on display sizes to create an intentional, cohesive editorial presence across hero units and product titles.
- **Inter (Body Copy):** Handles descriptions, ingredient decks, routine guides, and specifications. Neutral geometry ensures complex cosmetic nomenclatures remain crisp and effortless to parse.
- **Scale Discipline:** High-contrast headings step down systematically on mobile viewports via responsive tokens to preserve layout balance and eliminate awkward line breaks in multi-word beauty claims.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model relies on a responsive fluid grid governed by generous vertical cadence to let cosmetic imagery breathe:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed max container width of `1280px` with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` exterior margin padding. Product showcase grids default to 4 columns (or 3 columns with an expanded filter drawer).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid boundaries with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins. Product catalog surfaces collapse into a 2- or 3-column flow.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Compact fluid view using `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Carousels employ horizontal snap scrolling with 16px peek margins to encourage tactile discovery.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Micro-paddings strictly align to an 8px base rhythm (`space-sm` at 8px, `space-md` at 16px, `space-lg` at 24px) to ensure unified component breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate ambient diffusion rather than dark drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Canvas):* Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale porcelain wash (`#FFF0F3`).
  - *Tier 1 (Cards, Product Tiles):* White (`#FFFFFF`) elevated by a 1px border of `#FCE7F3` and ambient petal diffusion.
  - *Tier 2 (Modals, Floating Carts, Flyout Drawers):* Elevated pure white surfaces cast with layered blurs.
- **Ambient Shadow Character:** 
  - *Subtle Glow:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(238, 114, 157, 0.08)` creates an ethereal, healthy radiance under floating badges and hover states.
  - *Elevated Overlay:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(238, 114, 157, 0.06)`.
- **Glassmorphic Elements:** Top navigation bars and sticky checkout bottom-bars use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` framed with a bottom rule in `#FCE7F3`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a pill-shaped, ultra-soft form factor (`roundedness: 3`). Rounded geometries mimic organic fluid shapes, cream cushions, and smooth cosmetic packaging:

- **Pill Forms (`rounded-full`):** Applied universally to primary call-to-action buttons, tag chips, counter badges, navigation pills, and active filter tabs.
- **Curved Containers (`rounded-3xl` / `rounded-2xl`):** Product media cards, diagnostic quiz dialogs, promotional banners, and informational panels maintain generous curvature (`1.5rem` to `2rem`).
- **Form Controls:** Form inputs, selects, and text fields use a refined `rounded-xl` or full-pill profile to preserve ergonomic touch comfort.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background in `#EE729D`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-md`. Border-radius is `9999px`. Resting state incorporates an ambient pink bloom shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(238, 114, 157, 0.35)`). Hover transition shifts background to `#FF758F` with a soft `translate-y(-1px)`.
- **Secondary / Soft Pill:** Background in `#FFF0F3`, text `#EE729D`, border 1px solid `#F4CCD3`. Hover state shifts background to `#FFE5EC`.
- **Ghost Action:** Borderless, text in `#374151`, underline transition with rose hover accent.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- **Status & Marketing Badges (Best Seller, New):** Uppercase `label-sm`, full pill rounding, padding `4px 10px`. Background `#FFF0F3`, text `#EE729D`, subtle 1px border in `#F4CCD3`.
- **Trust & Certification Badges (BPOM Certified, Halal):** Compact pill container. Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`, accompanied by a tiny 12px checkmark or certified shield icon.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** White backdrop wrapped in a 1px border of `#FCE7F3` with `1.5rem` corner radius. Overflow hidden.
- **Media Canvas:** Pale neutral background (`#FAF7F8` or `#FFF0F3`) with centered cosmetic packaging photography. Top-left corner houses floating pill badges; top-right hosts a floating circular wish-list toggle (`36px` diameter).
- **Content Area:** Padding `space-md`. Displays category kicker (`label-sm` in `#EE729D`), product title (`headline-sm` in `#1F2937`), 5-star rating cluster with review counts (`body-sm` in `#4B5563`), followed by price emphasis. Hovering triggers a subtle lift and brings a secondary "Quick Add" pill button into view.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Pill or `1rem` rounded frames with `1px` border in `#FCE7F3` and background in `#FFFFFF`. Text styled in `body-md` (`#1F2937`). Focus ring uses a smooth 2px outer aura in `rgba(238, 114, 157, 0.25)` and border color shifts to `#EE729D`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px` custom controls. Unchecked state: border 1.5px solid `#F4CCD3`. Checked state: background `#EE729D`, white indicator icon, with gentle scale animation (`scale(1.05)` bounce).

### Lists & Accordions (Ingredients & Usage)
- Collapsible ingredient trays bordered with `#FCE7F3`. Accordion headers feature bold clean typography (`headline-sm`) paired with a circular plus/minus icon toggle rotating 45 degrees upon expansion. Content reveals clinical benefits cleanly separated by soft horizontal rules.
